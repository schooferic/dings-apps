Support

Questions, bugs or requests about WürfelDings and YatzyDings.

Contact

Just send me an email at kontakt@ericschoof.com. I answer as quickly as I can – these apps are a hobby project, so it may take a few days.

For bug reports it helps to know: your iPhone model, your iOS version, the app version (shown in the app's settings) and what exactly you did before the problem appeared.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find finished games?

Under "Previous games" on the home screen. It holds the games of the last 30 days, including the turn history and the winner.

I mistyped a score – can I undo it?

Yes. Tap "Undo" during the game to take back the last entry.

Can I resume an interrupted game?

Yes. Open "Previous games" and select the game – you will be asked whether you want to continue playing.

Can I change the target score?

In WürfelDings, yes: the target score and minimum entry score are in the settings. The defaults are 10,000 and 250 points.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Both apps work entirely offline and all data stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy for details.

Are the apps available for iPad or Android?

Not at the moment. Both apps are made for iPhone.
