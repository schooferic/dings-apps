Privacy Policy

For the apps WürfelDings and YatzyDings. Last updated: 23 August 2026.

In short

Neither app collects, stores or transmits any personal data to me or to third parties. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. The apps never connect to the internet.

What the app processes

The apps store only what you enter yourself, so that they can do their job:

  • the player names you choose,
  • the scores you enter and the history of your games,
  • your settings, such as language, appearance and target score.

This data stays on your device only. It is never sent to a server, because the apps have no networking capability at all. As the developer, I never have access to it.

Deletion

You can delete individual games and saved player names inside the app. Removing the app from your device deletes all of its data along with it.

Backups

If you use iCloud backups or encrypted device backups, app data may be included in those backups. Apple's privacy policy applies to them; I have no access to them.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Since I neither collect nor store any of your data, I hold no data these rights could apply to. You are welcome to contact me at the address above with any questions, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

Changes

If the functionality of the apps changes, I will update this policy and the date shown above.
