Roll. Laugh. Don't do maths.

Two small iPhone apps that take exactly one job off your hands on game night: keeping score. So you can focus on what matters – whose turn it is, and who has earned the last beer.

WürfelDings

For Ten Thousand. Collect ones and fives, roll again, lose everything. The app keeps count.

YatzyDings

The whole scorepad on your iPhone. Upper section, lower section, bonus – the sums take care of themselves.

Why bother

Because the paper always goes missing. Because everyone wants someone else to keep score. Because by the end three people have three different totals and nobody wants to add it up again. And because the evening is too good to spend on mental arithmetic.

You still roll real dice. That is the whole point.

How it goes

  1. Type in the names.
  2. Play. Enter your score after each turn, that's it.
  3. The app adds up, warns you about mistyped numbers and notices when someone has won.
  4. Afterwards, look back at who was on a roll – and who lost again.

What isn't in there

No ads. No purchases. No account, no login, no email address. The apps don't even have an internet connection – everything stays on your iPhone.

Who is behind this

Me, Eric Schoof. These two apps are a hobby project alongside my studies. They exist because on game night the score sheet had gone missing for what felt like the hundredth time, and nobody felt like doing the maths again.

Free, and staying that way. Nothing to buy, nothing to unlock, no ads. I earn nothing from them and don't want to – I just want to support good times with friends!
